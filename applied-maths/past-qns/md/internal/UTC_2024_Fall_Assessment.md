#### 1.
a) Show that the necessary condition for a function $f(z) = u(x, y) + i v(x, y)$ to be analytic in a domain $D$ is $u_x = v_y$ and $u_y = -v_x$ at each point. **[8]**

b) Define Laplace equation. If $u = 3xy + x^2 - y - y^2$, show that there exists a harmonic conjugate function $v(x, y)$ such that $f(z) = u + i v$ is analytic. **[7]**

---

#### 2.
a) State and prove Cauchy's Integral Formula. Evaluate the integral:

$$
\oint_C \frac{e^{2z}}{z^2 - 1} \, dz
$$

where $C$ is the ellipse $4x^2 + (y-2)^2 = 4$. **[8]**

**OR**

State and prove Cauchy's Residue Theorem. Evaluate:

$$
\oint_C \frac{e^z}{(z-1)^3} \, dz
$$

where $C: |z-i| = 1$. **[8]**

b) Define conformal mapping. Determine the region in the $w$-plane under the transformation $w = 3z$ corresponding to the region in the $z$-plane enclosed by the lines $y = 1, y = 2, x = 1, x = 2$. **[7]**

---

#### 3.
a) Solve the difference equation:

$$
y_{n+2} + 6y_{n+1} + 9y_n = 2^n
$$

with $y_0 = 0$, $y_1 = 0$ by using Z-transform. **[8]**

b) Find the Z-transform of $\sin \omega t$. **[3.5]**

c) Find $Z^{-1}\left[ \frac{z^2+2z}{(z-1)^2} \right]$. **[3.5]**

---

#### 4.
a) If $f(n) = 0$ for $n < 0$ such that $Z(f(n)) = F(z)$, prove the time-delay theorem (second shifting theorem):

$$
Z(f(n-k)) = z^{-k} F(z)
$$

where $n > 0$, $k > 0$. **[8]**

b) Derive the two-dimensional wave equation with necessary assumptions. **[7]**

**OR**

A uniform rod of length $L$ has its ends maintained at $0^\circ\text{C}$ and the initial temperature of the rod is $u(x, 0) = 3 \sin\left(\frac{\pi x}{L}\right)$ for $0 < x < L$. Find the temperature $u(x, t)$ at any point and any time $t$. **[7]**

---

#### 5.
a) Find the deflection $u(x, y, t)$ of a square membrane of side $a = b = 1$ with $c = 1$, if the initial velocity is zero and the initial deflection is $k \sin(\pi x) \sin(\pi y)$. **[8]**

b) Solve the partial differential equation:

$$
\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2} - \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial y^2} = 0
$$

using the method of separation of variables. **[7]**

---

#### 6.
a) State and prove the Convolution Theorem for Fourier transform. **[8]**

b) Find the Fourier integral representation of the function:

$$
f(x) = \begin{cases} 
e^{-ax}, & x > 0 \\ 
e^{ax}, & x < 0 
\end{cases}
$$

where $a > 0$. **[7]**

---

#### 7. Short Questions: **[4 x 2.5 = 10]**
a) Express the function $f(z) = \cosh z$ in the form $u + i v$.
b) Find the Z-transform of $n a^n$.
c) Show that the Fourier cosine transform is linear: $\mathcal{F}_c\{a f(t) + b g(t)\} = a \mathcal{F}_c\{f(t)\} + b \mathcal{F}_c\{g(t)\}$.
d) Solve the partial differential equation $u_y = u_x$.
