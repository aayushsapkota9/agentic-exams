#### 1.
a) Define an analytic function. State and prove the necessary conditions (Cauchy-Riemann equations) for $f(z)$ to be analytic. Also, check whether $f(z) = \sin z$ is analytic or not. **[8]**

**OR**

Define a harmonic function. If a function $f(z) = u + i v$ is analytic, show that $u$ and $v$ satisfy the Laplace equation (i.e., $u_{xx} + u_{yy} = 0$ and $v_{xx} + v_{yy} = 0$). **[8]**

b) Define conformal mapping. Name the types of conformal mapping. Translate the rectangular region $ABCD$ in the $z$-plane bounded by $x = 1, x = 3, y = 0, y = 3$ under the transformation $w = z + (2 + i)$. Show with a figure. **[7]**

#### 2.
a) State Cauchy's Integral Formula for derivatives. Evaluate:
$$\oint_C \frac{e^z}{(z-1)^3} \, dz$$
where $C: |z-1| = 3$. **[8]**

b) Find the Laurent series expansion of $f(z) = \frac{1}{(z-1)(z-2)}$ in the region $|z| < 1$. **[7]**

#### 3.
a) State and prove the Second Shifting Theorem of Z-transform. Obtain the Z-transform of $(1 - e^{-an})^2$. **[8]**

b) Solve the difference equation using Z-transform:
$$y_{n+2} - 3y_{n+1} + 2y_n = 4^n$$
with $y_0 = 0$, $y_1 = 1$. **[7]**

#### 4.
a) Find the Fourier cosine transform of $f(x) = e^{-mx}$ for $m > 0$. **[8]**

b) What are the Fourier sine and cosine integrals of a function $f(x)$? Represent:
$$f(x) = \begin{cases} 
\cos x, & |x| < \frac{\pi}{2} \\ 
0, & |x| > \frac{\pi}{2} 
\end{cases}$$
as a Fourier integral, and hence show that:
$$\int_0^\infty \frac{\cos\left(\frac{w\pi}{2}\right) \cos xw}{1 - w^2} \, dw = \begin{cases} 
\frac{\pi}{2} \cos x, & |x| < \frac{\pi}{2} \\ 
0, & |x| > \frac{\pi}{2} 
\end{cases}$$
**[7]**

#### 5.
a) Solve the wave equation $\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial t^2} = c^2 \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2}$ with the boundary conditions $u(0, t) = 0, u(L, t) = 0$ and initial deflection $u(x, 0) = f(x)$, initial velocity zero. **[8]**

b) A homogeneous rod of conducting material of length $100\text{ cm}$ has its ends kept at zero temperature, and the initial temperature is:
$$u(x, 0) = \begin{cases} 
x, & 0 \le x < 50 \\ 
100 - x, & 50 \le x < 100 
\end{cases}$$
Find the temperature $u(x, t)$ at any point $x$ and time $t$. **[7]**
