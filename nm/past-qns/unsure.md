### Overview
All questions extracted across all 14 past examination papers (28 images) have been definitively identified, transcribed, and classified into their respective chapters (Chapters 1 to 6) following the official Pokhara University syllabus.

No questions were missed or left unassigned.

In several examination papers, Pokhara University deviated from the canonical numbering pattern (e.g. assigning a Chapter 1 root-finding question to 2(a), a Chapter 2 interpolation question to 1(b), a Chapter 3 integration question to 2(b), or a Chapter 4 linear systems question to 3(b)). ## 2025

* **Fall - Question 1(b):** Estimating number of students with weight between 60 and 70 lbs from a grouped frequency table.
  - *Syllabus Rationale:* Interpolation with unequal/equal intervals (Lagrange / Newton's interpolation).
  - *Assigned Chapter:* **Chapter 2 (Interpolation and Approximation)**
* **Fall - Question 2(b):** Evaluate $\int_0^{\pi/2} (1 + 3\cos 2x) \, dx$ using Simpson's $1/3$ and $3/8$ rule.
  - *Syllabus Rationale:* Numerical Integration (Simpson's rules).
  - *Assigned Chapter:* **Chapter 3 (Numerical Differentiation and Integration)**
* **Spring - Question 3(b):** Solve $27x + 6y - z = 85$, $x + y + 54z = 110$, $6x + 15y + 2z = 72$ using Gauss-Seidel method.
  - *Syllabus Rationale:* Iterative methods for systems of linear equations.
  - *Assigned Chapter:* **Chapter 4 (Solution of System of Linear Algebraic Equations)**
* **Spring - Question 6(a):** Typo in original university paper printing $\frac{d^2x}{dx^2} = 6xy^2 + y$ with initial conditions $y(0) = 1, y'(0) = 0$.
  - *Correction & Rationale:* Typo for $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2} = 6xy^2 + y$, solved by RK-4 method for higher order ODEs.
  - *Assigned Chapter:* **Chapter 5 (Solution of Ordinary Differential Equations)**
